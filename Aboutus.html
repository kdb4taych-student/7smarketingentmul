Our History

Founded in 2025, our company has quickly grown into a trusted digital partner for businesses looking to build a strong online presence.

We specialize in creating professional websites and managing impactful social media strategies that help our clients stand out and connect with their audiences.

Since our establishment, we’ve successfully worked with over 10 clients across various industries — helping them design, develop, and grow their digital platforms with measurable results.

Our mission is to empower brands through creativity, innovation, and strategic digital solutions. We believe every business deserves a strong online identity that reflects its vision and drives real growth.

Together, let’s bring your digital ideas to life.

Director of 7s Marketing Enterprise
Tay Chee Hai

The director began his professional journey in 2019 as a Computer Technician at Advanced First Computer Sdn. Bhd., building strong technical and problem-solving skills.

In 2020, he started improving his communication abilities by studying English at Touch English Centre, while continuing his IT career until 2023.

Later that year, he pursued his passion for creativity and business growth by studying Digital Marketing at Suma College, where he learned to combine technology with marketing strategy.
After graduating from both Touch English Centre (2024) and Suma College (2025), he founded 7s Marketing, a digital marketing agency dedicated to helping brands grow through innovation and data-driven solutions.

 -From technology to creativity — building a new generation of digital marketing.
